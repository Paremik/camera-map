# Coordinate verification

A record is not independently verified simply because its coordinate was imported from ITS or OSM. Building/site positions and exact camera mounting points are different claims.

For each reviewed point record:

- Catalogue ID and exact coordinate being evaluated.
- Public source URL and archived reference where permitted.
- Review date, reviewer and method (e.g. dated owner installation plan, independently identifiable mount).
- Accuracy: exact mount, approximate site, or unresolved.
- Evidence of the camera's existence and a separate decision about optical parameters.

Only evidence supporting the coordinate's stated accuracy can change its verification flag. Optical verification requires its own evidence. No new mount positions were independently verified in this maintenance change; the verified filter remains honest.
